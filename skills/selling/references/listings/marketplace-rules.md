# Marketplace seller rules — verified 2026-11-12

## Rozetka (sellerhelp.rozetka.com.ua)
**Title** — https://sellerhelp.rozetka.com.ua/p217-product-title.html (page updated 12.06.2026)
- Formula: Тип товару · Бренд · Модель · Колір · Характеристики · (Артикул). Type starts with a capital letter and answers "what is it".
- Max 255 characters; optimal for SEO up to 60.
- No two products with identical titles in your list; brand in the title = producer field.
- No punctuation (commas, dots, dashes) except inside model names; capital first letter, not all caps (brand/model/size in caps allowed); replace special symbols (λ Ω ™ ½ @ ^ *) with text.
- Ukrainian or Russian, English words allowed for brand/model; XML: Russian in `<name>`, Ukrainian in `<name_ua>`; Russian-only titles are auto-translated.
- Clothes: size mandatory; children's clothes: height.
- Banned: marketing words (Від виробника, Оновлена версія, Покращений, Топовий, Акція, Знижка, Розпродаж, Новинка…), attention symbols, store name or URLs, "Original/Оригінал" (except phone/tablet/laptop parts and printer consumables), jargon. Non-compliant titles are not published.

**Photos** — https://sellerhelp.rozetka.com.ua/p216-product-images.html (updated 13.10.2026)
- 1–15 photos; 3–6 recommended. 400×400 min, 4000×4000 max, 1000×1000 optimal; zoom needs ≥ 720×630. JPEG, GIF, PNG, WebP; ≤ 10 MB; RGB/sRGB. Image URL: HTTPS only, no Cyrillic or spaces, ≤ 1999 chars.
- From 31.01.2027 Google Shopping ads need ≥ 500×500 px.
- Main photo: product without packaging, not cropped, correct proportions, nothing covering it, front view visible; no selfies or collages; must match the category's standard look. Extra photos in rotation order, lifestyle at the end.
- Background: white/light plain or transparent recommended; pastel plain allowed; bright saturated colours banned; product should fill most of the frame (background ≤ 20% recommended). Clothing and large items may be shot outdoors under conditions.
- Banned: marketing badges (хіт, топ продажів, акція, подарунок, безкоштовна доставка), web links, foreign graphics, IP-infringing images, money (unless sold), smoking, sexual content, warranty info or contacts, black-and-white filters. First photo should be clean with no watermark — text on the main image removes the item from Google Shopping ads.

**Description** — https://sellerhelp.rozetka.com.ua/p212-product-description.html (updated 16.09.2026)
- ≤ 50,000 characters; must match title, photos and attributes; no grammar errors.
- Banned: links, other products/assortment, price/bonuses/discounts, images, video, emoji, delivery and payment info, contacts and store info, additional services, unrelated info, coloured text, importer details.
- XML: `<description>` (RU) and `<description_ua>` (UA) with HTML in CDATA; allowed tags: strong, b, p, br, h1–h6, ul, ol, li, table, tbody, tr, td, th, div, span.

**Commission:** by category in the seller's tariff — not verified; read the seller cabinet. A 2021 Forbes interview stated Rozetka did not register dropshipping stores `[Unverified]` current status.

## Prom (support.prom.ua)
https://support.prom.ua/hc/uk/articles/360017589018 — "Правила оформлення товарів та послуг"
- **Title:** full name, brand/model, key characteristics, most important first; users may see only the first ~70 characters; max 110. Banned: meaningless strings, non-standard symbols and emoji, SEO words (купити, продати, оптом), keyword lists, region names, contact info, all-caps text, variant characteristics (size, colour) when the item is a variant.
- **Photos:** ≤ 10 MB; JPG, GIF, PNG; max side 1280 px (larger are compressed); adult goods retouched. Banned: photos you don't own, weapons/ammunition (even in background), non-matching photos, placeholders, foreign logos/brands, contacts/QR codes, subjective claims ("Краща пропозиція на ринку"). Clothes & shoes: 3+ photos recommended (Prom says such items sell ~2× better than with one photo).
- **Description:** up to 50,000 chars; key info in the first 160–500 characters; minimum recommended 30 chars; characteristics go to the attribute block, not the text. Banned: copied text, contacts or calls to order by phone/messengers, links to other sites/products, keyword lists, all-caps, incoherent text, HTML that affects page functionality.
- **Price and stock:** UAH (or enable currency conversion); no outdated or 1 UAH prices; "В наявності" only if shipping ≤ 3 days, otherwise "Під замовлення" with a truthful term.
- **Search words:** must match the item; "купити/замовити/region" are added automatically — don't add them.
- **Category:** most specific; each category has a minimum price — wrong category can hide your price as "understated"; "…загальна" categories get less traffic.
- **Characteristics:** fill at least 2–3 main ones; Prom states ~3× higher conversion with filled characteristics; up to 70 custom characteristics (not used in filters).

## Epicentr marketplace (epicentrk.ua/ua/seller/)
- Seller status: FOP group 2 or 3, or a legal entity; acquiring required; delivery via Epicentr pickup points (ЦВЗ) and Epicentr postomats is mandatory.
- Fees: 120 UAH/month (incl. VAT) cabinet fee; account activation needs a 1,001 UAH top-up (1,000 stays as balance for commissions); commission 5–25% by category, charged only on completed orders, refunded if the buyer cancels.
- No dropshipping: goods must be in stock or made to your existing designs.
- Content rules (title/photo limits): not verified on 2026-11-12 — open the seller help from the cabinet ("Довідка Епіцентр Маркетплейс") and record them here with a date.
