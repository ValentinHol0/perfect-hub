# Ukrainian customs and import tax — what was verified (2026-11-12)

This is orientation, not advice. Laws are changing; read the current text and confirm with a customs broker and an accountant.

## Parcels (international postal and express shipments to private persons)
- Today: goods addressed to one private recipient in one dispatch with total invoice value up to EUR 150 pay no customs payments; above EUR 150 (up to EUR 10,000), 10% duty on the excess and 20% VAT on the excess plus duty. Example (EUR 200 item): excess 50 → duty 5 → VAT 11 → ~EUR 16 to pay. Sources: Customs Code art. 374, https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/4495-17 ; explainer https://news.dtkt.ua/taxation/pdv/108483-miznarodnii-soping-bez-siurpriziv-mitni-pravila-ta-podatki-u-2026-roci . customs.gov.ua's "Митниця і пошта" page blocks automated fetch — open it in a browser.
- Operators may consolidate several parcels to one recipient arriving together; the threshold then applies to the total (secondary source above).
- Change adopted 15.10.2026: bill No. 16051-1 (Tax Code) and No. 15460 (Customs Code) passed as a whole — VAT on goods under EUR 150 bought on foreign platforms, collected by the platform; non-commercial P2P parcels under EUR 45 stay exempt; the norms start no earlier than 01.07.2027 after a Cabinet decision. Sources: https://suspilne.media/1428115-posilki-z-za-kordonu-do-150-evro-opodatkuut-pdv-rada-uhvalila-zakon/ ; https://epravda.com.ua/finances/rada-ostatochno-uhvalila-zakonoproyekt-pro-opodatkuvannya-posilok-826591/ . Check signature, publication and the Cabinet decision before relying on dates.
- Impact on product choice: today Temu/AliExpress parcels under EUR 150 arrive without VAT, which is part of why the "Temu twin" kills COD offers (C2). After the change, platform prices for UA buyers should include 20% VAT — re-check parity then, do not pre-empt it.

## Commercial import (stock for resale)
- Customs value is normally the transaction value plus transport to the Ukrainian border; duty = customs value × rate for the UKTZED (УКТЗЕД) code; import VAT 20% on (customs value + duty + excise if any). The calculator follows this order. Rates by code: use the official tariff (Customs Tariff of Ukraine) or the broker's classification — `[Unverified]` until the code is confirmed; many consumer goods carry 0–12% `[Unverified]`.
- VAT payers can usually credit import VAT (`importVatReclaimable: true`); single-tax payers without VAT registration cannot — it is a cost.
- Broker fees and terminal/warehouse charges per declaration: take the quote; divide by units.

## Taxes after import (for unit economics)
- FOP group 3 (2026): 5% of income without VAT or 3% + VAT; 1% military levy on income; income cap 1,167 minimum wages ≈ 10,091,049 UAH. Secondary source: https://buhplatforma.com.ua/news/109482-fop-3-grupa-u-2026-rotsi-limity-ta-podatky — confirm on tax.gov.ua.
- Settlement transactions with consumers generally require PRRO/RRO fiscal receipts, including COD — `[Unverified]` specifics; route to `perfect-hub:business`.
