# ФОП deadline calendar

The calendar is generated by `tax-calc.mjs` from `deadlineRules` in `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/data/fop-rates.json` — never typed by hand.

## 1. Run
```bash
node ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/tax-calc.mjs calendar --group 3 --year 2026 --ics deliverables/business/calendar/fop-g3-2026.ics
node ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/tax-calc.mjs calendar --group 2 --year 2027 --json
node ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/tax-calc.mjs calendar --group general --year 2026
```
The calendar lists every event **dated within the year** (so January–February items for the previous year's Q4 or annual return are included). Amounts appear only when that period's year block exists in the data file; otherwise the event shows no amount — never fill it in from memory.

## 2. Rules the script applies (verify against `sources.md` when in doubt)
| Who | What | Deadline | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Groups 1–2 | ЄП advance + military levy for the current month | by the 20th of the same month (may be prepaid up to the year end) | ПКУ 295.1; п. 16-1 пп. 1.11 |
| Groups 1–2 | Annual declaration (incl. ЄСВ annex) for the previous year | 60 calendar days after 31 Dec | ПКУ 296.2, 49.18.3 |
| Group 3 | Quarterly declaration (Q4 incl. ЄСВ annex) | 40 calendar days after quarter end | ПКУ 296.3, 49.18.2 |
| Group 3 | ЄП (5 %/3 %) + military levy (1 %) for the quarter | 10 calendar days after the filing deadline | ПКУ 295.3; п. 16-1 пп. 1.11 |
| All ФОП | ЄСВ for the quarter | by the 20th of the month after the quarter | Закон 2464-VI ст. 9 ч. 8 |
| General system | PIT advances | 20 April, 20 July, 20 October | ПКУ 177.5.1 |
| General system | Annual declaration (with ЄСВ) | by 1 May | ПКУ 177.11, 49.18.4 |
| General system | Annual PIT payment | 10 days after the filing deadline `[Unverified: ПКУ 57.1 vs 179.7 (1 August)]` | ПКУ 57.1 |

**Weekends.** Filing deadline on a weekend → next working day (ПКУ 49.20); tax payment deadline on a weekend → next operational day (ПКУ 57.1, last paragraph). The ЄСВ law has no shift rule → the script keeps the date and notes "pay by the previous working day". The 10-day payment period is counted from the unshifted filing deadline (conservative) `[Unverified]`.

**Holidays.** Under martial law art. 73 КЗпП (holidays as days off) is not applied (Закон 2136-IX ст. 6 ч. 6); whether a holiday still shifts a tax deadline is `[Unverified]`, so the script shifts only Saturdays/Sundays. Add verified dates to `nonWorkingDays` in the data file if needed.

## 3. Deliver
1. Table: date · what · period · amount (or "depends on income — run `calc`") · basis · note (shift).
2. `.ics` file in `deliverables/business/calendar/` — all-day events with a reminder 3 days before; import: Google Calendar → Settings → Import; Apple Calendar → File → Import; Outlook → Add calendar → Upload from file.
3. Personalise: group 1–2 amounts use the maximum rate — replace with the local council's rate (`tax-calc.mjs calc --rate`). Group 3 payment amounts come from `calc` with the quarter's income from `references/bookkeeping.md`.
4. Out of scope (say so): payroll reporting and taxes for employees, VAT returns (monthly, ПКУ 202.1), excise, land/property taxes, local fees — recommend the accountant or the ДПС tax calendar `[Unverified: current ДПС calendar URL]`.
5. Dispatch `business-accountant-check` with the command and output; end with the verify line from the `business` skill.
