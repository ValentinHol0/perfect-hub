# Page blueprints and the copy deck

Blueprints are an order of jobs, not a template. Drop sections that have no evidence behind them; never pad.

## 1. Landing page (cold or warm traffic, one offer)
| # | Section | Job | Copy rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero | In 5 seconds: what it is, for whom, why care, what to do | Headline = outcome for this reader (≤ 10 words); subhead = how / mechanism / for whom (≤ 25 words); one CTA with a specific verb; one proof line (rating with source, named client, number with source); visual shows the product in use |
| 2 | Problem | Show you understand the reader's situation | Their words from reviews/interviews; 2–4 concrete moments, not a list of fears |
| 3 | Mechanism | Why this works where alternatives failed | One idea; a diagram or 3 steps; no jargon without explanation |
| 4 | Offer | Exactly what they get, price, terms | What is included, price in UAH, delivery/payment terms, guarantee; bonuses only if real |
| 5 | Proof | Make the promise believable | Reviews with name + context + permission; before/after with conditions; numbers with source and date |
| 6 | Objections / FAQ | Remove the last blockers | Real questions in customer wording, answered with facts; delivery, payment, returns, compatibility, data |
| 7 | Final CTA | Ask again at the decision point | Restate the promise in one line + the same CTA intent + risk reversal microcopy |

Length follows awareness and risk: a 349 грн impulse product for a most-aware visitor can stop after section 4; a 30 000 грн course for a problem-aware visitor needs all 7.

## 2. Product page (e-commerce)
Above the fold: product name as people search for it (brand + type + key attribute), price with any discount shown honestly (old price only if it was really charged — a fake "was" price can be misleading advertising under Law "Про рекламу", art. 10), availability, delivery estimate ("Відправимо сьогодні, якщо замовите до 16:00" — only if true), payment options (накладений платіж, оплата частинами — only those actually offered), primary CTA "Купити" / "Додати в кошик".
Below: benefits in use (not the spec sheet), specs table, size or compatibility guide, reviews, delivery/returns/warranty block, related items. Photos: real product, scale reference, in-use shot.

## 3. Lead-gen / service page
Hero promise + who it is for + the one form. Form: ask only what the first call needs (name, phone or Telegram, one qualifying question); say what happens next and when ("Передзвонимо протягом робочого дня" — only if staffed). Proof: cases with numbers and named clients (permission), process in 3–5 steps, price range or "from" price when possible (a hidden price filters nobody out, so expect more unqualified leads — a practitioner heuristic worth testing, not a measured fact).

## 4. Pricing section
Plans named for the user ("Для одного магазину"), not for the tier ("Pro"); one recommended plan; the difference between plans in one line each; annual saving shown in UAH, not only %; FAQ on billing, cancellation, refunds; CTA per plan.
