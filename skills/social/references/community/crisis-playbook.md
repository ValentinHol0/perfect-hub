# Crisis playbook

Times are house targets to agree with the owner in advance; write the names and phone numbers of the escalation chain into this file for each brand.

## 1. Severity levels
| Level | Signals | Owner | First response |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 — routine | single complaints, normal tone | community manager | per reply principles |
| 1 — issue | several similar complaints in a day (same defect, same delay), one angry post with traction | community manager + responsible team (logistics, product) | same day; acknowledge publicly where it appears; fix the cause |
| 2 — crisis | complaints spreading beyond own channels (Telegram channels, media, influencers), safety or legal angle, data leak, insensitive post that went viral | owner / director + SMM lead (+ lawyer) | within 1 hour: pause scheduled posts, holding statement once facts are confirmed |
| 3 — severe | harm to people, regulatory action, war-related or national-tragedy context, large-scale fraud using the brand | owner + lawyer + PR | within 1 hour; all communication approved by the owner and lawyer |

## 2. First-hour checklist (level 2+)
1. **Pause** all scheduled posts and ads on every platform (including Telegram scheduled messages and ad campaigns via `/ads` owner).
2. **Capture**: screenshots with timestamps and links of the original issue and the biggest reactions.
3. **Facts**: what happened, since when, how many people affected, what is already done — from the responsible team, not from comments.
4. **Decide the owner** of the response and one approver; nobody else posts or replies.
5. **Holding statement** (only verified facts, no speculation) on the channel where it started + pinned.
6. **Monitor**: brand name and handle in Telegram search, Instagram and TikTok search, Google News, review sites; log volume every hour.
7. **Internal note** to all staff: what to say if asked (one paragraph) and "do not comment personally".

## 3. Holding statement template
«Ми знаємо про {проблема} і розбираємося. Уже зробили: {дія}. Наступне оновлення — {час/дата} тут, у {канал}. Якщо це стосується вашого замовлення, напишіть нам у {канал_підтримки}.»
Rules: no blame, no minimising («поодинокі випадки»), no legal admissions without the lawyer, no humour, no emojis. Publish the update at the promised time even if there is little news.

## 4. Full statement (when facts are clear)
What happened → who was affected → what we did → what changes so it does not repeat → what affected people get (refund, replacement, compensation — as approved) → how to reach us. Signed by a real person with a role.

## 5. Sensitive context (Ukraine)
- Mass-casualty attacks and national mourning: pause promo and entertainment the same day; review posts scheduled for the next 48 hours; no "business as usual" jokes.
- War-related missteps (insensitive humour, symbols, Russian-language promo, cooperation with sanctioned or Russian-linked partners): treat as level 2+; apologise specifically, remove the content, explain the change.
- Fundraising and donations: report real amounts and receipts; never imply donations that did not happen.
- Fake accounts and scams using the brand: warn followers, report to the platform, consider reporting to the cyber police (cyberpolice.gov.ua) `[verify the current reporting channel]`.

## 6. After the crisis (within a week)
Post-mortem: timeline · first signal and when it was noticed · response times · what worked · what failed · root cause · changes (process, product, policy, filters) · owner and deadline per change. Share a short version with the team; update this playbook and the moderation rules.
