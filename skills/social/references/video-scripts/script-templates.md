# Short-video formats and script templates

Timings are planning targets for a 20–40 s video; scale beats, not the hook.

## 1. Formats
| Format | Beats (time) | Best for | Filming |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mistakes list | hook 0–2 · mistake 1 2–9 · mistake 2 9–16 · mistake 3 16–23 · payoff 23–27 · CTA 27–30 | saves, expertise | one location, close-ups per mistake |
| Tutorial / how-to | result first 0–2 · steps (one per 3–5 s) · result again · CTA | saves | top-down or over-the-shoulder, phone on a tripod |
| Before → after (real) | after 0–1 · before 1–3 · process 3–20 · after reveal · CTA | product proof | same angle and light for before/after |
| Myth vs fact | myth on screen 0–2 · why people believe it · fact + proof · takeaway | shares, comments | talking head + B-roll |
| POV | «POV: …» situation 0–2 · escalation · twist/payoff | reach, relatability | acted scene, natural phone angle |
| Behind the scenes | intriguing process shot 0–2 · steps of making/packing · people · finished product | trust, community | real workplace, real staff (consent) |
| Answer a comment | comment sticker/screenshot 0–2 · answer with demo · CTA to ask more | community, comments | reply-to-comment feature |
| Unboxing / what arrives | parcel (Nova Poshta box) 0–2 · opening · contents · first use | e-commerce | real parcel, hands only |
| Series episode | series title card ≤ 1 s + hook · episode content · «Наступна серія — …» | follows, return viewers | same frame and opener every episode |
| Founder talking head | strong opinion line 0–2 · story · lesson · CTA | B2B, personal brand | eye-level, window light, clip-on mic |
Trend adaptation: keep the trend's structure, change the content to the brand's expertise; skip trends that need humour inappropriate for the moment (mourning days, tragedies).

## 2. Stories sequence (3–7 frames)
1. Hook frame (question or bold statement) → 2–4. Value or behind the scenes (one point per frame) → 5. Interaction (poll, quiz, question box, slider) → 6. Proof (real review screenshot with permission) → 7. CTA (link sticker or "Напишіть у Direct").
Text inside the 9:16 safe zone; one sticker per frame; ≤ 20 words per frame.

## 3. Subtitles — SRT format
```
1
00:00:00,000 --> 00:00:01,800
Ваша кава гірчить
через ці три речі

2
00:00:01,800 --> 00:00:04,200
Перша — окріп
```
Rules: ≤ 2 lines, ≤ 32 characters per line (house rule for vertical video), cue 1–3 s, break lines at phrase boundaries, keep names and terms spelled correctly; save as UTF-8.

## 4. Filming checklist (phone)
- 4K or 1080p, 9:16 vertical, 25 or 30 fps (match the editor and platform), clean lens.
- Light: face to a window or a soft LED; no strong backlight unless intended.
- Sound: clip-on mic for talking heads; record 3 s of room tone; avoid echoing rooms.
- Framing: key content inside the central safe area; leave space at the bottom for captions and UI.
- Shoot B-roll for every spoken point; shoot the hook 3 ways.
- Consent: people on camera agree (written for staff and customers); no children without parents' consent.
- Brand: product clean, labels facing camera, no competitor products visible unless intended.

## 5. Repurposing one video
Reels (native text, licensed audio) → TikTok (native captions, TikTok text tool) → Shorts (title + same file) → Telegram (video post or video circle teaser + link) → Stories (first 15 s + link sticker). Export without other apps' watermarks.
