# Occasions to plan around (Ukraine)

Public holidays come from `scripts/data/ua-holidays.json` (Labour Code Art. 73, https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/322-08, checked 2026-11-12). Everything below is planning guidance: add the dates you use to `--extra` after checking them for the year.

## 1. Public holidays (in the data file)
New Year (1 Jan) · International Women's Day (8 Mar) · Labour Day (1 May) · Day of Remembrance and Victory over Nazism (8 May, memorial) · Constitution Day (28 Jun) · Ukrainian Statehood Day (15 Jul) · Independence Day (24 Aug) · Defenders Day (1 Oct) · Christmas (25 Dec) · Easter and Trinity (Sundays, computed from the Julian Paschalion; Trinity = Easter + 49 days).
Martial law: Art. 73 does not apply while martial law is in force (Law No. 2136-IX, Art. 6, https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2136-20) — the days are working days. Content tone still matters.

## 2. Memorial and sensitive dates (no promo, no humour)
| Date | Occasion | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 24 February | anniversary of Russia's full-scale invasion (2022) | fact; plan remembrance or silence |
| 8 May | Day of Remembrance and Victory over Nazism in WWII (public holiday) | in the data file |
| 4th Saturday of November | Day of Remembrance of the Victims of the Holodomors | per Ukrinform citing presidential decrees of 1998 and 2007 `[verify the date for the year]` |
| 29 August | Day of Remembrance of the Defenders of Ukraine | `[Unverified]` — check the presidential decree |
| National mourning days | declared after attacks with mass casualties | follow official announcements; pause scheduled promo posts the same day |
Rule: when a mass-casualty attack happens, pause scheduled posts for the day and review the next 48 hours; the community manager has the authority to do this without approval.

## 4. Planning rhythm around a sale
−14 days: content that creates need (education, problems) · −7: teaser · −3: reveal terms · day 0: launch post + stories every few hours · middle: proof (real reviews, sold-out sizes) · last day: deadline reminder · +1: thank-you and results (only real numbers).
