## 2. Collection checklist per competitor
- [ ] Homepage and main landing pages fetched; hero quoted; date recorded.
- [ ] Pricing page fetched; snapshot saved (PDF/screenshot in `deliverables/research/<topic>/snapshots/`) or archived copy URL.
- [ ] Delivery, payment, returns, guarantee pages read.
- [ ] Search position checked for the 3–5 main queries (location and date noted).
- [ ] Social profiles: follower counts and posts in the last 30 days, with date.
- [ ] Ad libraries searched by page/advertiser name; number of active ads, oldest start date, angles.
- [ ] Reviews: sources, count read, date range, theme tally, 1–2 short quotes per theme.
- [ ] Registry/aggregator record for the legal entity (if Ukrainian).
- [ ] Genuine trial or purchase notes (if done): steps, time to value, friction.
