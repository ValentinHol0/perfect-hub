# Meta ad policy pitfalls for e-commerce (checked 2026-11-12)

Primary source: Meta Advertising Standards, https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/ — open the specific policy before deciding; quote it briefly with the link. TikTok and Google have their own policies; check them separately (`references/tiktok.md`, `references/google.md`).

## Health and wellness (https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/health-wellness/, change log 22.07.2026)
- Weight-loss/gain products and services, dietary and health supplements, cosmetic products/procedures/surgeries → target **18+**.
- Not allowed for weight-loss/gain or cosmetic products: statements of inferiority about appearance (negative attacks on appearance, body parts, hygiene); for weight products, close-ups pinching fat; claims that results come solely from wearables.
- No skin-whitening products causing permanent change.
- No claims to cure listed incurable diseases (diabetes, herpes, thyroid, psoriasis, Ebola, cancer, autism, Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, MND, HIV) — symptom management claims are a different matter.
- No clickbait in a health or weight context: sensational claims, specific outcomes within a set time without qualifiers.
- Allowed when 18+: before-and-after transformations for general cosmetic products/procedures; dietary weight products showing usage and time to noticeable results.
- Age restrictions do not apply to general well-being (fitness equipment), general food, non-permanent cosmetics (creams, make-up, hair products), dental products.
Correction to a common myth: "Meta bans before/after" is not a blanket rule — it depends on the category and targeting above.

## When an ad is rejected
1. Read the exact policy in the notice (Account Quality).
2. Fix the ad and the landing page to comply; don't resubmit unchanged copies or cloak — repeated violations restrict the account.
3. Request review only when the ad complies; keep a record (screenshot, date, policy name).
4. If a whole category is restricted, change the angle (product function instead of body result), age-gate, or choose another channel.
