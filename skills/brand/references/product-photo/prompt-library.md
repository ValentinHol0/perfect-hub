# Prompt library — sets, light, mood, banners

Phrases are building blocks for the art-direction paragraph (SKILL.md §3). Pick one per category; describe in sentences.

## 1. Sets and surfaces
| Set | Phrase | Suits |
|---|---|---|
| Stone pedestal | "on a solid cylindrical beige stone pedestal with a fine matte texture" | cosmetics, fragrance, jewellery |
| Acrylic block | "on a clear polished acrylic block with soft refractive edges" | tech accessories, skincare |
| Raw natural | "on a rough basalt rock with a few dried grasses" | outdoor, natural care, coffee |
| Dark water | "on a still, mirror-like dark water surface with faint ripples" | fragrance, drinks, premium tech |
| Linen and wood | "on undyed linen over an oak tabletop" | food, ceramics, home goods |
| Seamless colour | "on a seamless paper backdrop in <brand hex>, floor curving into the wall" | catalogue, marketplace, bold brands |
| Kitchen in use | "on a worn wooden chopping board in a sunlit home kitchen" | kitchenware, food |
| Pure white | "isolated on pure white (#FFFFFF), soft contact shadow" | marketplace main images (check marketplace rules) |

## 2. Lighting
| Setup | Phrase | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Softbox | "soft diffused light from the top left, gentle falloff, realistic soft shadow" | clean commercial default |
| Rim / backlight | "strong backlight creating a thin bright outline, dark background" | premium, dramatic |
| Split | "hard key light from the side, half the product in soft shadow" | texture, depth |
| Window daylight | "natural window light from the right, warm morning tone" | lifestyle, food, home |
| Window shadows | "shadows of a window frame and leaves across the back wall" | organic, calm |
| Under-glow | "subtle floor reflection with a soft warm glow from below" | tech, fragrance |

## 3. Mood and texture
- "a thin layer of white mist rising from the bottom, dark grey background"
- "fine water droplets on the matte surface, macro detail"
- "warm low sun with long soft shadows"
- "crisp winter daylight, cool tones" (seasonal campaigns)

## 4. Camera
- Packshot: "100 mm macro, f/8, everything sharp" · three-quarter view: "50 mm, slightly above eye level".
- Hero with copy space: "35 mm, wide framing, product in the left third, right half empty".
- Flat lay: "top-down, 90°, even light, objects aligned to a grid".
- Lifestyle: "35–50 mm, natural perspective, shallow depth of field, real environment".

## 5. Ad and banner backgrounds (no text — copy is set in HTML)
| Theme | Phrase |
|---|---|
| Editorial | "asymmetric editorial composition, soft pastel backdrop, generous empty space on the right" |
| Tech showcase | "dark carbon-texture background with a few precise cyan light lines, product sharp in the foreground" |
| Action | "dynamic particles bursting behind the product, slight motion blur on the particles only, high-contrast light" |
| Seasonal UA | "golden wheat field at sunset, shallow depth of field" / "snowy Carpathian-style forest edge, cold blue hour" — avoid national symbols as decoration unless the brand has a reason |

## 6. Quality markers and negative constraints
- Add at most one short marker: "commercial product photography, sharp focus, realistic materials and reflections".
- Constraints sentence: "No text, no logos, no watermark, no extra products, no hands unless described."
- Avoid: keyword chains ("8k, ultra detailed, trending"), more than one light setup, conflicting styles.

## 7. Example (full paragraph)
"A 250 ml amber glass bottle of cold-pressed sunflower oil with a plain kraft label, standing upright in three-quarter view in the left third of the frame, the right half left empty for a headline. Shot on a 50 mm lens at f/5.6 from slightly above eye level. Natural window light from the right with a warm morning tone and soft shadows. The bottle sits on undyed linen over an oak tabletop, a few sunflower seeds scattered near its base. Palette: amber #C8811E, linen #EDE6D6, oak #8A6A48, deep green #20412F. Calm, honest, farm-to-table mood. No text, no logos, no watermark, no extra bottles."
Use a real photo of the actual bottle for anything presented as the product; this prompt suits a concept or background where the label is not read as real.
